# Evidence anchors

These are starting points, not an exhaustive bibliography. Prefer primary/current sources
when extending a mapping.

## Connectome

- MaleCNS project: full male Drosophila CNS connectome, v1.0 released 2026-06-08 and paper
  published 2026-09-03:
  https://male-cns.janelia.org/

## Reward, punishment and learning

- Waddell review, *Drosophila olfactory memory: single genes to complex neural circuits*.
  Reviews monoaminergic appetitive/aversive teaching signals and mushroom-body learning:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2098
- Liu et al., *A subset of dopamine neurons signals reward for odour memory in Drosophila*:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/nature11304
- Vasan et al. (2024), dopamine-mediated short/long-term memory dynamics; discusses PAM/PPL1
  reinforcement and recurrent DAN/MBON learning units:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07819-w
- *Dopaminergic systems create reward seeking despite adverse consequences*; demonstrates
  opponent appetitive/aversive dopaminergic effects and aversive effects from inhibiting
  reward-associated populations:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06671-8

## Social behavior / game-like interaction

- Yamamoto & Koganezawa, courtship circuits and P1:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn3567
- Anderson, internal states linked to mating/aggression:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn.2016.125
- Aggression/dominance review highlight:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2064
- 2026 closed-loop courtship adaptation highlight:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-026-01043-3

## Use rule

For an experiment-driving mapping, prefer evidence that manipulates the relevant neurons or
pathway and changes behavior/learning. Anatomical connectivity alone supports a weaker claim.


## Legal-process translation

The legal map is intentionally a computational analogy. It does not claim that litigation has a biological homolog. Experiment-driving use should ground reward/punishment/social-reliability mechanisms in Drosophila evidence from the RL/game-theory maps and treat legal stages as the external environment/state machine.


## Mechanosensation, audition, thermosensation and wearables

- Eberl et al. review of Johnston's organ development and active mechanics; JO is a major
  Drosophila auditory/mechanosensory organ sensitive to near-field courtship sound:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3417114/
- Review of the Drosophila auditory system; Johnston's organ projects into the antennal
  mechanosensory and motor center and supports hearing, gravity and wind sensing:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4007284/
- Tuthill & Wilson review of insect mechanosensation and adaptive motor control, including
  chordotonal proprioception and Drosophila Johnston's organ:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5120761/
- Review of Drosophila thermosensation and temperature preference; peripheral hot and cool
  cells project through dedicated thermal pathways:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10741703/
- Carta, Vestergaard & Poulet review of thermal encoding across mammals and insects,
  including Drosophila thermosensory circuits:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-025-01001-5

Human wearable locations and actuator modalities are engineered choices. These references
support the fly-side sensory mapping, not claims about human haptic safety thresholds.
