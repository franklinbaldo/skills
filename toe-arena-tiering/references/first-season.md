# First-season seeding notes

Initial public Arena seeding on 2026-09-17 should prefer a small, legible set over an encyclopedic dump.

Suggested opening field:

- String theory / M-theory — mature broad unification programme; no direct empirical confirmation.
- Loop quantum gravity — mature quantum-gravity programme; narrower unification scope than a full ToE.
- Asymptotic safety — mature UV-completion programme for gravity; active phenomenology, with central fixed-point and matter-completion questions still open.
- Causal dynamical triangulations — nonperturbative lattice approach with quantitative Planck-scale observables; principally quantum gravity rather than complete matter unification.
- Adrian P. C. Lim (2026), “From Loop Quantum Gravity to a Theory of Everything” — explicit recent ToE proposal combining Chern-Simons and Einstein-Hilbert structures; classify conservatively pending independent development and empirical contact.
- Franklin Baldo, “Time as Concatenation…” — exhibition/adjacent entry, not a ToE contender, because the paper explicitly says its scope is narrower than a replacement for physical time.

The first public season should allow tier S to be empty.
