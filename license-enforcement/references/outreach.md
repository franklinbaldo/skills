# Outreach protocol

External contact is the point where an internal hypothesis becomes someone else's problem.
Treat that transition as irreversible enough to require explicit human approval.

## Before drafting

Confirm that the decision packet states:

- target identity and public artifact;
- source material and frozen source version;
- strongest use evidence;
- strongest counterevidence;
- current license status (`licensed`, `unlicensed`, or `unknown`);
- whether the observed use appears operational;
- which factual or legal questions remain unresolved.

If the target's identity is uncertain, do not guess a company merely because a product name
looks similar.

## First contact: compliance inquiry

The first external message should normally be an inquiry, not a demand.

It should:

1. identify the sender and the relevant source repository;
2. identify the public target artifact precisely;
3. describe the apparent overlap neutrally and concretely;
4. state that Skill Use License 0.1 does not grant Operational Use without a separate paid
   license;
5. acknowledge that the sender may not have visibility into a private license or other
   authorization;
6. invite the recipient to identify an existing authorization or discuss regularization;
7. provide a reasonable response date approved by the human reviewer;
8. preserve all correspondence in the case record.

Avoid loaded language such as “theft”, “piracy”, “fraud”, or “willful infringement” unless a
human legal reviewer has a specific reason to use it.

## Regularization offer

A retroactive licensing proposal is an offer, not a declaration that the quoted number is
already due.

Separate clearly:

- prospective permission;
- any retroactive release/settlement;
- scope of skills/material covered;
- covered legal entity/entities;
- covered products/workflows;
- duration;
- price and currency supplied by the rights holder;
- payment mechanics supplied by the rights holder;
- whether attribution or notices remain required;
- what claims, if any, acceptance resolves.

Never infer a monetary amount from company size, revenue, funding, or perceived ability to pay
unless the rights holder has adopted an explicit pricing policy using that metric.

## Formal extrajudicial notice

Draft only after explicit approval to escalate. The notice should distinguish allegations from
established facts and should attach or identify the evidence relied on.

A useful structure is:

- sender / rights holder;
- recipient;
- licensed material and rights asserted;
- public evidence observed;
- applicable license terms;
- prior contact and response, if any;
- requested cure or proposed resolution;
- deadline approved by the human reviewer;
- reservation of rights;
- channel for response.

Do not state that a court has determined infringement when none has. Do not describe a proposed
license fee as a liquidated debt unless an applicable agreement or other valid legal basis
actually makes it one.

## Judicial or platform escalation

The skill may prepare an evidence packet for counsel or a human decision-maker. It must not
autonomously:

- file a lawsuit;
- submit a platform takedown request;
- contact a regulator;
- contact the target's customers, investors, employer, or press;
- publish an accusation;
- initiate debt collection.

Those steps can create consequences disproportionate to a mistaken match. The agent's useful
role is to make the human decision better documented, not to remove the human from it.
